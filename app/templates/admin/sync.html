Sync from Google Forms

Import student data and photos from a Google Sheets spreadsheet

Setup Instructions
  1. Create a Google Form with these fields: Full Name, Roll Number, College, Department, Semester, Student ID, Email, Phone, Front Photo, Left Photo, Right Photo.
  2. Google Forms automatically creates a Google Sheet with responses. Open it.
  3. Set the sheet to "Anyone with link can view" sharing, or share it with your service account email.
  4. Copy the Spreadsheet ID from the URL:
    ...spreadsheets/d/SPREADSHEET_ID/edit
  5. Set up a Google Service Account, download credentials JSON, and place it as google_credentials.json in the app root.
  6. Share the sheet with the service account email (Viewer access).
  7. Click Sync Now!
Credentials File

Place your Google Service Account credentials at:

google_credentials.json

Download from Google Cloud Console → APIs & Services → Credentials.
